How Problem2Impact works

A four-step journey from real problem to real-world implementation

📝
STEP 1

Post a Problem

Any citizen can describe their real-world challenge with evidence, location, and expected outcome.

🤖
STEP 2

AI Matches Teams

Our AI analyses skills needed and suggests the best-matched innovators from universities and industry.

💡
STEP 3

Teams Solve

Cross-university and industry teams collaborate, submit solutions, and get instant AI validation feedback.

🏆
STEP 4

Funded & Implemented

Government or industry finalizes the best solution, awards the team, and implements it in the real world.
